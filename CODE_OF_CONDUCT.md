# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, precise, and constructive. Discuss the technical merit, source quality, and placement of a contribution without attacking the contributor. Welcome corrections, disclose conflicts of interest, and distinguish evidence from opinion.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, spam, undisclosed paid promotion, and sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Scope and Enforcement

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or the integrity of the list.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact information in the owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
